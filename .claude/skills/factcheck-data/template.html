How this was checked

  • Every description and ou_involvement paragraph was split into separate claims. The numeric fields checked were tilt, rot_period, e, i, launch_year, status, landed_coords and the JUICE waypoints.
  • Confirmed: a primary or authoritative source says the same thing. Needs correction: broadly right but imprecise, overstated or out of date. Contradicted: a source says otherwise. Unverified: no public source found either way.
  • General science statements (for example "Venus rotates backwards") were checked against agency fact pages. Obvious textbook facts are grouped into one confirmed row per paragraph so the list stays usable.
  • Asteroid naming citations were checked word for word against the official citation text in the JPL Small-Body Database.
  • Not checked: radius and orbit size (display scale), texture and model paths, and image credits. The credits entry has no factual claims, but a quick look found a few that seem to be placeholders; see the last card on the Missing missions tab.
  • "Unverified" does not mean wrong. Many OU claims describe teaching use or internal work that is never published.
